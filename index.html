Investment Schemes

Investment pathways structured around investor profile and legal clarity

Each route is designed to keep asset security, operational simplicity, and regulatory compliance aligned with the profile of the investor.

Indonesian Investors SHM Route

Domestic Investment

Designed for Indonesian investors who want direct land ownership paired with professionally managed plantation participation.

  • Direct land ownership through Sertifikat Hak Milik (SHM).
  • Fully managed, hands-off plantation operations from planting to harvest.
  • Structured profit-sharing linked to plantation harvest cycles.
  • A real-asset position with potential long-term land value appreciation.
Foreign Investors PT PMA Route

Foreign Investment (PT PMA)

Built for international investors who require a compliant structure for agricultural asset control and legal participation in Indonesia.

  • Formation of a fully legal PT PMA company structure.
  • Land rights secured through Hak Guna Bangunan (HGB) under the investor's corporate entity.
  • Structured in line with BKPM and prevailing foreign investment regulations.
  • Can support discussions around Investor KITAS and selected long-stay visa pathways.
